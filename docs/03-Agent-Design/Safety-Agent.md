# Safety & Alerts Agent

## Purpose

Monitors trip disruptions and triggers escalation protocols.

---

## Responsibilities

* Detect simulated flight delays
* Monitor high-risk destination flags
* Trigger alerts
* Escalate emergencies

---

## Inputs

* Trip status
* Destination risk data
* Delay signals (simulated)

---

## Outputs

* Notification event
* Escalation level
* Emergency contact details

---

## Escalation Levels

1. Informational
2. Action Required
3. Emergency

---

## MVP Note

Alert triggers are simulated for demonstration.
