# Hotel Booking Agent

## Purpose

Evaluates accommodation options against policy constraints.

---

## Responsibilities

* Validate nightly rate
* Enforce star rating policy
* Check proximity rule
* Recommend best option

---

## Inputs

* Destination
* Stay duration
* Policy caps

---

## Outputs

* Approved option
* Cost estimate
* Policy compliance flag

---

## Decision Logic

* Rate ≤ policy cap
* Minimum star rating
* Distance from client site

---

## MVP Note

Simulated inventory, real validation logic.
