# Demo Flow

## Demo Scenario

Employee plans trip:
Pune → Bangalore
3 employees
2 days
Client Visit

---

## Step-by-Step Demo

1. User submits trip request
2. Orchestrator parses structured data
3. Policy validation triggered
4. Flight & Train comparison displayed
5. Cost optimization logic shown
6. Packing checklist generated
7. Nearby CRM accounts suggested
8. Safety information displayed
9. Explanation panel: "Why this recommendation"

---

## Edge Case Demo

Change team size to 5 → system re-optimizes travel mode.

Exceed budget → policy violation warning displayed.

Trigger emergency simulation → escalation workflow shown.

---

## Final Message

"This system reduces enterprise travel inefficiency through AI-driven orchestration, cost optimization, and policy intelligence."
